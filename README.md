# Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Microsoft Excel dashboard** designed to analyze retail sales performance across product categories, regions, customer segments, and shipping modes.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profit, quantity, and discount patterns to help identify key business trends and performance differences across various dimensions.

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the use of **Microsoft Excel for data analysis, visualization, and interactive dashboard development**.

The dashboard transforms retail sales data into an interactive analytical report using charts, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers.

The analysis focuses on:

- Sales performance
- Profit performance
- Quantity sold
- Discount distribution
- Regional performance
- Product category performance
- Customer segment performance
- Shipping mode performance

---

## Key Metrics

The dashboard analyzes the following business metrics:

- **Sales**
- **Profit**
- **Quantity Sold**
- **Discount**

These metrics are analyzed across different categories, regions, segments, and shipping modes.

---

## Key Insights

- **Technology** generates the highest sales among the three major product categories shown.
- **West** is the strongest region in terms of profit, followed by East, South, and Central.
- Sales volume varies considerably across regions, with the **West region recording the highest quantity sold**.
- The **Government customer segment** has the highest quantity sold among the customer segments displayed.
- **Standard Class** accounts for the largest share of sales among the available shipping modes.
- Discount distribution varies across product categories, with **Office Supplies accounting for the largest share** in the dashboard.
- The dashboard enables users to interactively filter the analysis by **Category** and **Region**.

## Dashboard Features

### Sales Analysis
Compares total sales across major product categories:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

### Regional Analysis
Analyzes:

- Quantity by region
- Profit by region

The dashboard allows users to compare performance across:

- Central
- East
- South
- West

### Profit Analysis
Examines profit across different product categories and customer segments to identify variations in profitability.

### Shipping Analysis
Shows the contribution of different shipping modes to total sales:

- First Class
- Same Day
- Second Class
- Standard Class

### Discount Analysis
Provides a category-wise view of discount distribution.

### Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- Category
- Region

These filters allow users to dynamically explore different parts of the dataset.

## Analytical Approach

The project followed a structured Excel analytics workflow:

1. **Data Preparation**
   - Reviewed the retail sales data.
   - Identified relevant fields for sales, profit, quantity, discounts, categories, regions, segments, and shipping modes.

2. **Data Analysis**
   - Used Excel-based analytical tools to summarize and compare business metrics.
   - Analyzed performance across multiple business dimensions.

3. **PivotTable & PivotChart Development**
   - Created summarized views of the data.
   - Converted analytical results into interactive visualizations.

4. **Dashboard Development**
   - Combined multiple charts into a single dashboard.
   - Added slicers for interactive filtering.

5. **Insight Generation**
   - Identified high-performing categories and regions.
   - Compared sales, profit, quantity, discounts, and shipping performance.
